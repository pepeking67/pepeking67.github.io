Policy Gradient Algorithm

It is used in RL problems with continuous action spaces. It directly learns the optimal policy by a parametric probability distribution πθ(a∣s)\pi_\theta(a|s)πθ​(a∣s), which stochastically selects action a in state s according to parameter θ\thetaθ. It typically proceeds by sampling this stochastic policy and adjusting θ\thetaθ in the direction of grater total reward, so there is no need for experience replay.

Markov Chain Monte Carlo(MCMC)

Sample a large number of trajectories using minibatch and take their average, which is an unbiased approximation. This is called Morkov Chain Monte Carlo.
Even though the gradient of the parametrized policy does not depend on r(τ)r(\tau)r(τ), depending on θ\thetaθ, this term adds high variance in MCMC sampling because of erratic trajectories. Instead, total reward r(τ)r(\tau)r(τ) of the trajectory τ\tauτ can be replaced by discounted return GtG_tGt​.
This PG algorithm is called REINFORCE.
