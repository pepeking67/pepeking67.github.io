## Course overview

University of Colorado Boulder's three-course specialization covering classical computer vision, deep learning for visual tasks, and modern multimodal models.

### Topics covered

- Image processing, feature extraction, and classical vision algorithms.
- CNNs, ResNet, U-Net, autoencoders, and GANs for recognition, segmentation, and generation.
- Transformers, Vision Transformers, CLIP, and diffusion models for visual and multimodal understanding.
