Projects

ROBOT FAILURE RECOVERY
In preparationOct 2026 —

Learning to Recover from Manipulation Failures

A solo project investigating how imitation-learned robot policies can recover from unexpected failures with limited additional experience.

Combining Diffusion Policy with residual reinforcement learning in simulation.

Project overview ↗

Goal. Study whether training with recoverable failure states improves recovery under a fixed additional interaction budget.

Planned approach. Train a Diffusion Policy from public demonstrations, freeze it, and learn an SAC-based residual policy using robosuite and robomimic. Develop the pipeline on Lift and conduct the main experiments on Can.

Planned evaluation. Compare residual policies trained from standard initial states and a mixture including failure states, measuring recovery, task success under perturbations, and normal-condition performance.

Role. Independent project; responsible for design, implementation, evaluation, and failure analysis.

Stage. Preparation. Experimental results will be added as the project develops.
