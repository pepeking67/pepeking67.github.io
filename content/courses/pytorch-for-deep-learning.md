## Course overview

DeepLearning.AI's three-course Professional Certificate on building, training, optimizing, and deploying deep learning models in PyTorch.

### Topics covered

- Tensors, neural networks, data loading, and complete training pipelines.
- Transfer learning and fine-tuning with TorchVision and Hugging Face; architectures such as ResNet and Transformers.
- Model compression and deployment, including pruning, quantization, ONNX, and MLflow.
