About

I am an undergraduate student at KAIST studying Computer Science and Mathematical Sciences.

My interests center on sample-efficient reinforcement learning and robot learning, particularly how agents can adapt to unfamiliar situations and recover from failures.

My reading spans offline-to-online reinforcement learning, visual representation learning, generative models, and world models & VLA, with a broader interest in reliable decision-making and generalization.

For my next project, I plan to study how a robot trained from demonstrations can recover from unexpected failures and resume a task with only a small amount of additional experience.
