# Ch. 1 Basic Concepts
$`G=(V,E)`$: graph → directed graph / undirected graph (symmetric).
For **undirected graph**, **degree** of a node ($`k_i`$): # of edges adjacent to node $`i`$.
$$
\overline k=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^Nk_i=\frac{2|E|}{N}.
$$
For **directed graph**:
- **In-degree** ($`k_i^{\mathrm{in}}`$): # of incoming links of node $`i`$.
- **Out-degree** ($`k_i^{\mathrm{out}}`$): # of outgoing links of node $`i`$.
Total degree of node $`i`$: $`k_i=k_i^{\mathrm{in}}+k_i^{\mathrm{out}}`$.
Average degree: $`\overline{k^{\mathrm{in}}}=\overline{k^{\mathrm{out}}}=|E|/N`$.
**Induced subgraph**: a subset of the vertices of the graph and all of the edges connecting pairs of vertices in that subset.
![](notion-file-block://03a3ba12-88aa-4c09-84f4-d9694d9603f3/f636cc84-5ccb-41ff-8e1c-a42e9a3b0e6c?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=ebf9cd42-e2ba-4924-b3d1-cda3b563c743.png)
**Ego-network** of a target node: an induced subgraph which is induced by its target node and its direct neighbors.
**Complete graph**: an undirected graph with $`|E|_{\max}`$ edges.
$$
|E|_{\max}=\binom N2=\frac{N(N-1)}2,\qquad\text{Average degree}=N-1.
$$
**Clique**: a subset of vertices of an undirected graph such that every two distinct vertices in the clique are adjacent.
⇒ Its induced subgraph is complete.
**Maximal clique**: a clique that cannot be extended by including one more adjacent vertex. (It is not a subset of a larger clique.)
![](notion-file-block://2672e949-99a5-4945-a413-a9b18bd755f5/9080258f-e4ac-49f7-8902-c1c9ca015954?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=d6dce76e-8588-43a7-878d-f3899968344c.png)
**Bipartite graph**: a graph whose nodes can be divided into two disjoint sets $`U`$ and $`V`$ s.t. every edge connects a node in $`U`$ and one in $`V`$.
![](notion-file-block://1447471a-16b7-4138-8bed-2c4f4bceee67/21dbbb96-a2cf-491d-b656-2e5def691b89?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-2b.png)
## Representing graphs
**Adjacency matrix**
$$
A_{ij}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if there is a link from node }i\text{ to node }j,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
$$
Note. Sparse graph: $`|E|\ll|E|_{\max}`$.
Directed: $`N(N-1)`$ / undirected: $`\binom N2`$.
**Edge lists / adjacency lists**
![](notion-file-block://3c96a3e4-418f-4dea-8ef8-52b4bc07a462/471def5e-d71c-404b-ab7c-9f89f2eeb1fd?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-2c.png)
## Connectivity
**Connectivity of undirected graph**: if every pair of vertices in the graph is connected, i.e. there is a path between every pair of vertices.
**Connected component**: an induced subgraph in which any two vertices are connected, and which is connected to no additional vertices in the rest of the graph.
Directed graph: **strongly connected** if every vertex is reachable from every other vertex. Strongly connected components form a partition.
**Clustering coefficient** of node $`A`$: the probability that two randomly selected friends of $`A`$ are friends with each other.
**Bridge** $`(A,B)`$ if deleting the edge causes $`A`$ and $`B`$ to be in different components.
**Local bridge** if $`A`$ and $`B`$ have no common friend. But there should exist some path $`A\to B`$.
**Span** of a local bridge $`(A,B)`$: distance of $`A`$ to $`B`$ if the local bridge $`(A,B)`$ is removed.
# Ch. 2 Centrality Measures
How can we define the importance?
## ① Degree centrality
$`\#`$ of adjacent nodes.
## ② Betweenness centrality
Compute all-pairs shortest-paths in a graph.
(모든 쌍 최단 경로 문제 ⇒ Floyd–Warshall algorithm) ← 찾아보기!!
**Node betweenness**: # of times a node lies on the shortest path between other nodes.
⇒ Shows which nodes are bridges between nodes in a network.
**Edge betweenness**: # of shortest paths passing through the edge.
⇒ Bridges and local bridges have high betweenness.
## ③ Closeness centrality
Average length of the shortest path between the node and all other nodes in the graph. ← node's.
## ④ Hubs & Authorities
Idea: Receiving many links → receiving collective endorsement (보증).
⇒ If there is a link from $`p`$ to $`q`$, then $`p`$ confers authority on $`q`$.
We desire pages (relevant and authoritative, voting by in-links).
Ex. query: “newspaper” (newspaper 검색했을 때 무슨 페이지에 자료들이 어떻게 나오려나…)
Let $`S`$ be the set of web pages returned to be relevant.
간단한 버전:
1. Extract the subgraph induced by $`S`$ and its neighbors.
2. Count in-links to pages as authority score.
3. Hub score = out-link의 authority score 합.
4. New authority score = $`\sum\mathrm{hub}`$.
**Authority score**: the sum of incoming weights. (Initial = 1.)
**Hub score**: the sum of authority scores that the page points to.
**Normalization**: we only care about relative sizes.
## HITS algorithm
Idea: Good hub points to good authority page.
Let the relevant subgraph $`G=(V,E)`$, $`|V|=n`$.
Hub score $`h_p`$, authority score $`a_p`$.
$$
\sum_{p=1}^na_p^2=1,\qquad\sum_{q=1}^nh_q^2=1.\tag{1}
$$
1. Initialize $`h_p=1`$, $`\forall p`$.
2. Update
$$
a_p\leftarrow\sum_{q:(q,p)\in E}h_q,\qquad h_p\leftarrow\sum_{q:(p,q)\in E}a_q.
$$
Normalize $`a_p`$ & $`h_p`$ according to (1).
![](notion-file-block://5bbf1dc3-c171-48e2-b6b9-4eb372afc36b/8b039623-f668-4204-aa57-a22d89b61152?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-4a-final.png)
### Convergence
$$
A_{ij}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }(i,j)\in E,\\0&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
\qquad h\leftarrow Aa,\quad a\leftarrow A^Th.
$$
$$
h^{(0)}\Rightarrow a^{(1)}=A^Th^{(0)},\quad h^{(1)}=Aa^{(1)}
\Rightarrow h^{(1)}=AA^Th^{(0)}\Rightarrow h^{(k)}=(AA^T)^kh^{(0)}.
$$
**Note. Singular value decomposition (SVD)**
$$
A=U\Sigma V^T,\quad U^TU=UU^T=I,\quad V^TV=VV^T=I.
$$
$$
AV=U\Sigma\Rightarrow Av_i=u_i\sigma_i\quad(\Sigma\text{가 diagonal}).
$$
$$
AA^T=U\Sigma^2U^T=\sigma_1^2u_1u_1^T+\cdots+\sigma_n^2u_nu_n^T,
$$
$$
(AA^T)^k=U(\Sigma^2)^kU^T=(\sigma_1^2)^ku_1u_1^T+\cdots+(\sigma_n^2)^ku_nu_n^T.
$$
$$
h^{(k)}=(AA^T)^kh^{(0)}=(\sigma_1^2)^ku_1(u_1^Th^{(0)})+\cdots+(\sigma_n^2)^ku_n(u_n^Th^{(0)}).
$$
![](notion-file-block://9bc45838-d031-479d-bd84-ca032f1c11ba/6e4c2884-68d1-4971-ba39-ee0ee088b278?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=2b2b54ab-63d1-4061-aabe-e0dd9a7d2068.png)
We only care about direction of $`h^{(k)}`$.
$`h^{(k)}`$ converges to $`u_1`$. Rate: $`\sigma_2^2/\sigma_1^2`$.
Similarly, $`a^{(k)}`$ converges to $`v_1`$.
## ⑤ PageRank
Idea: $`p\to q`$ gives importance to $`q`$. (Just use one score for every page.)
**Endorsement**: passing directly from one prominent page to another.
![](notion-file-block://a41d0ec3-f9e2-4873-9a3b-6082045963f4/c647964e-73e0-4eb5-9309-316a7de5b910?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-6a.png)
**Ex.**
![](notion-file-block://713e5689-3e2d-4749-8523-fd9f7be97a39/fc14ff05-e154-4782-93c6-a8d1aa6cabb7?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-6b.png)
PageRank:
$$
x_q\leftarrow\sum_{p:(p,q)\in E}\frac{x_p}{|F_p|}.
$$
$`F_p`$: set of out-links from page $`p`$.
$$
P_{ij}=\begin{cases}\dfrac1{|F_i|}&(i,j)\in E,\\0&\text{no links from }i\to j,\end{cases}
\qquad x^{(k+1)}=P^Tx^{(k)}.
$$
$$
P=D^{-1}A.
$$
$`A`$: adjacency matrix. $`D`$: diagonal matrix of outdegrees, $`D_{ii}=\sum_{j=1}^nA_{ij}`$.
$`P`$ is a **row-stochastic** matrix: $`Pe=e`$.
Every row sums to 1. $`e`$ is a right eigenvector of matrix $`P`$ with eigenvalue 1.
$$
x^{(k+1)}=P^Tx^{(k)}.
$$
PageRank vector is the left eigenvector of $`P`$ with eigenvalue 1.
($`x`$: left eigenvector of $`P`$ = right eigenvector of $`P^T`$.)
### Two issues with PageRank
1. Dangling nodes (no out-links).
2. Sinks.
![](notion-file-block://3c730ab5-7c7e-4d58-993a-e98cc3a56b63/a06f3cbf-5afe-47fc-8976-ec77eed6e7df?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-7a.png)
Solution: add links to all nodes in the graph from every node.
$$
P\Rightarrow\overline P=\alpha P+(1-\alpha)\frac1nee^T\quad\text{: Google matrix},\qquad\overline P^Tx=x.
$$
**Markov property**
∃! PageRank vector ($`>0`$) when Google matrix is **stochastic** and **irreducible**.
- Aperiodicity, the power method will converge to this PageRank regardless of the starting vector for the iterative process.
### Random walks (Random Surfer Model)
Web surfer starts at a random page. With probability $`\alpha`$, he follows out-links with equal probability, and with $`1-\alpha`$, he jumps to a random page.
Stationary distribution is the probability of landing at pages.
### Power method
Initialize $`x`$ as $`\dfrac{1-\alpha}{n}e`$.
For each node $`v`$:
![](notion-file-block://18c6347a-796e-4805-85c8-514a6fd79f00/f010d665-656f-484b-b118-971fe5ffa8ab?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-7b-final.png)
Repeat criteria: ① predefined max reached, ② $`\|x^{(k+1)}-x^{(k)}\|_\infty<\varepsilon`$ ← predefined tolerance.
### Data-driven PageRank
Focus subset of nodes need to be updated. (worklist)
1. Initialize worklist as the entire vertex set.
2. Pick a node from the worklist (pop), and compute its PageRank. If updated, all its outgoing nbrs are added to worklist. If difference $`<\varepsilon`$, do not update.
3. Repeat until worklist becomes empty.
4. Normalize so that sum = 1.
### Convergence of data-driven PageRank
Assume there is no self-loop.
Initialize PageRank vector $`x`$ as $`(1-\alpha)e`$.
Update:
$$
x=\alpha P^Tx+(1-\alpha)e,\qquad(I-\alpha P^T)x=(1-\alpha)e.
$$
**Residual**
$$
r:=(1-\alpha)e-(I-\alpha P^T)x=\alpha P^Tx+(1-\alpha)e-x.
$$
$$
x^{(0)}=(1-\alpha)e,
$$
$$
r^{(0)}=(1-\alpha)e-(I-\alpha P^T)(1-\alpha)e=(1-\alpha)\alpha P^Te\ge0.
$$
$$
x_v^{(k+1)}=(1-\alpha)+\alpha\sum_{w\in S_v}\frac{x_w^{(k)}}{|J_w|}=x_v^{(k)}+r_v^{(k)}.
$$
이 과정에서 subscript는 벡터의 요소… (즉, 스칼라).
We can show that $`x^{(k+1)}\ge0`$.
**Proof.** Let $`\gamma=r_v^{(k)}`$. Then
$$
x^{(k+1)}=x^{(k)}+\gamma e_v.
$$
$`e_v`$: $`v`$-th position is 1.
$$
\begin{aligned}
r^{(k+1)}&=(1-\alpha)e-(I-\alpha P^T)x^{(k+1)}\\
&=(1-\alpha)e-(I-\alpha P^T)(x^{(k)}+\gamma e_v)\\
&=r^{(k)}-\gamma(I-\alpha P^T)e_v.
\end{aligned}
$$
⇒ $`v`$-th component of $`r^{(k+1)}`$ → 0. And we only add positive values to the other components.
So initial residual is positive ⇒ $`r^{(k+1)}\ge0`$.
$$
e^Tr^{(k+1)}=\begin{cases}e^Tr^{(k)}-r_v^{(k)}(1-\alpha)&J_v\ne\varnothing,\\e^Tr^{(k)}-r_v^{(k)}&J_v=\varnothing.\end{cases}
$$
Thus, any step decreases the residual by at least $`\gamma(1-\alpha)`$, and moves $`x`$ closer to the solution.
# Ch. 3 Closeness Measures
## Neighborhood overlap between A and B
$$
\frac{\#\text{ of nodes nbrs of both }A\text{ and }B}{\#\text{ of nodes nbrs of at least }A\text{ or }B}.
$$
Can be considered as an edge centrality measure.
모두 바로 연결된 노드끼리 비교해서, 물론 멀리 떨어져도 상관 ×.
## Personalized PageRank (PPR)
Let predefined set $`Q`$. Start by choosing a page at random. With probability $`\alpha`$, follow an outgoing link at random. With $`1-\alpha`$, randomly jump to nodes in $`Q`$.
Stationary distribution is the probability of landing at pages → PPR.
$$
x=\alpha P^Tx+\frac{1-\alpha}{n_Q}e_Q,\qquad n_Q=|Q|,\qquad
 e_Q(i)=\begin{cases}1&i\in Q,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
$$
The PPR vector will be biased towards the predefined set.
PPR vector를 closeness between the predefined nodes and the others로 해석 가능!
PPR ↑ ⇒ close to $`Q`$.

## Katz measure
$`A_{ij}^k`$ represents the number of paths from node $`i`$ to $`j`$ where the length $`k`$.
Similarity between node $`i`$ and $`j`$ := # of paths of various lengths between them.
$$
P_{\mathrm{Katz}}=\beta A+\beta^2A^2+\cdots=\sum_{i=1}^\infty\beta^iA^i,\qquad\beta<\frac1{|\lambda_{\max}|}.
$$
$`\beta`$: attenuation factor.
Truncated Katz:
$$
P_{\mathrm{tKatz}}=\beta A+\cdots+\beta^kA^k=\sum_{i=1}^k\beta^iA^i.
$$
⇒ Link prediction (close nodes will be likely connected).
How to speed up? (It is hard to compute $`A^k`$ on large network.)
## Low-rank approximation
**Note. Eigen decomposition**
$$
Ax=\lambda x\quad(\lambda:\text{eigenvalue},\ x:\text{eigenvector}).
$$
$`A=A^T`$ ⇒ $`\forall\lambda`$ are real, it has full set of mutually orthogonal eigenvectors.
$$
Av_1=v_1\lambda_1,\ldots,Av_n=v_n\lambda_n
\Rightarrow AV=V\Lambda,
$$
$$
V=[v_1\ v_2\ \cdots\ v_n],\quad\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n),\quad
V^TV=I,\quad VV^T=I.
$$
$$
A=V\Lambda V^T\quad\text{(Eigen decomposition)},\qquad A=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_ix_ix_i^T.
$$
$`\|x_i\|=1`$, eigenvectors of $`A`$.
# Ch. 4 Graph Clustering
## Girvan–Newman algorithm
Intuition: high betweenness edges tend to connect nodes far from each other, i.e. they tend to be local bridges between clusters.
1. Compute betweenness for all edges.
2. Remove the edge whose betweenness is the highest.
3. Continue until no edges left or stopping criteria.
## Modularity optimization
![](notion-file-block://6760946d-5cda-447d-81ac-06eb56293926/bb8e17e7-b208-4c70-99fd-a586b7d40989?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-10a.png)
Where $`A_{ij}`$: weight of the edge between $`i`$ and $`j`$.
$$
k_i=\sum_jA_{ij},\quad c_i:\text{community to which }i\text{ is assigned},
$$
$$
\delta(c_i,c_j)=1\text{ if }c_i=c_j\text{ and }0\text{ otherwise},\qquad m=\frac12\sum_{ij}A_{ij}.
$$
### Random configuration model
Cut each edge into two halves (each half-edge: stub). Rewire randomly with same degree distribution but uniformly random connections.
Expected # of edges between $`i`$ and $`j`$ of degrees $`k_i`$ and $`k_j`$:
$$
k_i\cdot\frac{k_j}{2m}=\frac{k_ik_j}{2m}.
$$
If $`Q>0.3`$, then partition is regarded to have a significant community structure.
⇒ Modularity can be used as metric that measures the quality of community structure.
We can identify communities by maximizing modularity.
### Louvain method
Greedy algorithm that optimizes modularity. Assign a different community to each node.
1. For each node $`i`$, $`i`$의 neighbor $`j`$에 대해서 $`i`$를 $`j`$의 community로 옮겼을 때 얻는 modularity 계산 → 가장 최대가 되는 곳으로 $`i`$ 이동 (gain $`>0`$). If no positive gain, $`i`$는 그대로… Local maxima of modularity를 얻으면 이 과정 반복 멈춤.
2. Build a new network whose nodes are the communities found in the first phase. (Community 자체를 하나의 node로 취급.) 다시 phase 1 적용.

## Clustering ← unsupervised
### ① K-means clustering
데이터들 ($`X`$) → $`k`$개 클러스터로 ($`C_1,\ldots,C_k`$).
$$
C_i\cap C_j=\varnothing\quad\forall i\ne j\quad(\text{disjoint}),\qquad C_1\cup\cdots\cup C_k=X\quad(\text{exhaustive}).
$$
Objective function:
$$
\min_{\{C_j\}_{j=1}^k}\sum_{j=1}^k\sum_{x_i\in C_j}\|x_i-m_j\|^2,\qquad
m_j=\frac{\sum_{x_i\in C_j}x_i}{|C_j|}.
$$
Limitation: separates by hyperplanes.
### Kernel method
![](notion-file-block://6043f694-c514-47b6-a521-1e9636ed5aaa/cd1b7868-f835-40a5-8a01-6c4a4851282f?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-12a.png)
Kernel trick: data only appear as inner product.
$$
K(x_i,x_j)=\varphi(x_i)^T\varphi(x_j).
$$
Kernel matrix: $`K=(K_{ij})_{i,j=1}^n`$, $`K_{ij}=\langle\varphi(x_i),\varphi(x_j)\rangle`$.
Symmetric (**positive semidefinite**) by Mercer's thm.
$`\forall x,\ x^TAx\ge0`$ → $`A`$ is p.s.d. ⇔ $`\forall`$ eigenvalues $`\ge0`$.
$$
A=V\Lambda V^T\Rightarrow x^TAx=x^TV\Lambda V^Tx=y^T\Lambda y=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_iy_i^2.
$$
For any $`M^TM`$ is symmetric p.s.d.
### ② Kernel K-means
⇒ We can have nonlinear boundary.
**Weighted K-means**
$$
\min_{\pi_1,\ldots,\pi_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\sum_{x_i\in\pi_c}w_i\|x_i-m_c\|^2,\qquad
m_c=\frac{\sum_{x_i\in\pi_c}w_ix_i}{\sum_{x_i\in\pi_c}w_i}.
$$
![](notion-file-block://96466616-0672-4b77-8ccd-c079db5119c9/3904c7f1-1ec2-40d9-8efc-dc8ac10d5246?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=3a2ec359-04f8-4b85-b70b-d11e6b4f56ad.png)
### Kernel K-means — objective function
![](notion-file-block://3086f082-5764-46e9-a922-e83c61081f19/ecd0746c-ca4e-49b4-a429-eac779837aad?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=535aa75c-b51f-46e5-91e6-30fb18552250.png)
$$
u_c\in\{0,1\}^{n\times1},\qquad u_c(i)=\begin{cases}1&x_i\in\pi_c,\\0&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}\qquad W=\operatorname{diag}(w_1,\ldots,w_n).
$$
$$
\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}w_j=u_c^TWu_c,\qquad
\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}\sum_{x_i\in\pi_c}w_iw_jK_{ij}=u_c^TWKWu_c,\qquad(WKW)_{ij}=w_iw_jK_{ij}.
$$
① constant.
$$
\text{② }\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{-2u_c^TWKWu_c}{u_c^TWu_c},\qquad
\text{③ }\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{u_c^TWKWu_c}{u_c^TWu_c}.
$$
⇒ Objective:
$$
\min_{u_1,\ldots,u_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{-u_c^TWKWu_c}{u_c^TWu_c}
=\max_{u_1,\ldots,u_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{u_c^TWKWu_c}{u_c^TWu_c}.
$$
### ③ NEO K-means (Non-Exhaustive, Overlapping)
Assignment matrix: $`U=[u_{ij}]_{n\times k}`$, $`u_{ij}=1`$ if $`x_i\in C_j`$, 0 otherwise.
![](notion-file-block://d0431ad7-e038-48b0-b9ae-2281213e8f12/6389a116-7f20-4cd7-8c5f-954dfd7e3eb9?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-13b.png)
$`\alpha`$: overlap, $`\beta`$: non-exhaustiveness.
![](notion-file-block://027b3494-aa06-4939-8aed-4061aec78f68/0caa4053-d5dc-48df-8fb2-d5e3ec154121?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-13c.png)
⇒ $`(1+\alpha)n`$ assignments are made & at most $`\beta n`$ data can have no membership in any cluster.
클러스터가 포함하는 데이터 수 합 = $`n+\alpha n`$, 클러스터의 데이터 개수 = $`n-\beta n`$.
∴ $`n+\alpha n-(n-\beta n)=\alpha n+\beta n`$: 겹치는 데이터 개수.
Let $`u_c`$ be the $`c`$-th column of $`U`$.
Weighted kernel K-means objective:
$$
\max_{u_1,\ldots,u_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{u_c^TWKWu_c}{u_c^TWu_c}
$$
$$
\text{s.t. }\operatorname{trace}(U^TU)=(1+\alpha)n,\qquad\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb I\{(U\mathbf1)_i=0\}\le\beta n.
$$
반복:
1. For each $`x_i`$,
$$
\operatorname{dist}(x_i,\pi_c)=K_{ii}-\frac{2\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}w_jK_{ij}}{\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}w_j}
+\frac{\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}\sum_{x_\ell\in\pi_c}w_jw_\ell K_{j\ell}}{(\sum_{x_j\in\pi_c}w_j)^2}.
$$
1. Assign $`n-\beta n`$ data.
2. Make $`\alpha n+\beta n`$ assignments by taking $`\alpha n+\beta n`$ minimum distances.
### ④ Graph clustering
Vertex set $`V`$, edge set $`E`$, weight set $`A`$ with $`a_{ij}`$.
**Graph cut**: partition $`V`$ into $`k`$ clusters of nodes s.t. sum of weights of between-cluster edges are minimized.
$$
\operatorname{Cut}(V_1,V_2)\equiv\sum_{i\in V_1,j\in V_2}a_{ij}.
$$
이 값을 그냥 최소화시키면 두 vertex 간의 균형이 안 맞을 수 있음.
$$
\min_{V_1,V_2}\operatorname{Cut}(V_1,V_2)\quad\text{s.t. }|V_1|=|V_2|.
$$
**Kernighan–Lin heuristic**
1. Starts with partitioning $`V_1`$ & $`V_2`$.
2. Calculate change in cut if two vertices are swapped.
3. Swap when decrease most.
![](notion-file-block://bfed53e0-aa3c-4048-858c-d4702b4d8576/8715a2a3-1016-4432-8432-786fccf7a895?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-14a.png)
![](notion-file-block://4d9dfd4a-afd7-47d2-b3e0-c16ed732d23b/ec1892f0-58b3-44be-af38-fdc19f33432b?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-15a.png)
⇒ Minimizing between-cluster edges = maximizing within-…
Associate an indicator vector $`y_c\in\{0,1\}^{n\times1}`$, $`c=1,\ldots,k`$, for each cluster.
$$
y_c(j)=\begin{cases}1&j\in V_c,\\0&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}\qquad
y_c^Ty_c=|V_c|,\quad y_i^Ty_j=0\text{ if }i\ne j.
$$
$`D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)`$, $`d_i`$: degree of node $`i`$.
$$
y_c^TDy_c=\deg(V_c),\qquad y_c^TAy_c=\operatorname{links}(V_c,V_c).
$$
![](notion-file-block://9a5eb937-f42c-4ac7-bdbb-7c4c0efdad17/c6d3916f-cb24-4f11-80ad-e9e312c780ed?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-15b.png)
**Note. Graph Laplacian**
$`L`$ is symmetric positive semidefinite.
$$
L\mathbf1=(D-A)\mathbf1=0.
$$
$`\#`$ of connected components = # of 0 eigenvalues of $`L`$.
$$
\forall z,\quad z^TLz=z^TEE^Tz=\frac12\sum_{(i,j)\in E}a_{ij}(z_i-z_j)^2.
$$
![](notion-file-block://a0c0c823-f0ca-4b35-8e1a-4a580d884569/efa24f6e-7829-4795-936d-eed33e77c539?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-15c.png)
$$
\begin{aligned}
NC(G)&=\min_{V_1,\ldots,V_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{\operatorname{Cut}(V_c,V\setminus V_c)}{\deg(V_c)}
=\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{y_c^T(D-A)y_c}{y_c^TDy_c}\\
&=k-\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{y_c^TAy_c}{y_c^TDy_c}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad\max_{y_1,\ldots,y_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{y_c^TAy_c}{y_c^TDy_c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
### Normalized cut for overlapping clustering
기존의 방식에서는 between-cluster edge가 penalized twice된다.
![](notion-file-block://34e29ab8-8b0c-4d27-b5e7-eac80ffa2b5d/0346909c-ed5f-4c2b-a856-2c8f8847d93d?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=401c1eaf-f5f0-4138-9795-e5590b0882b5.png)
즉, 기존에 between-cluster edge에 penalizing 하던 걸 그대로 하면 됨. Overlap만 고려하면 됨. ($`y_i^Ty_j\ne0`$ for $`i\ne j`$.)
$$
NC(G)=\max_{y_1,\ldots,y_k}\sum_{c=1}^k\frac{y_c^TAy_c}{y_c^TDy_c}
$$
$$
\text{s.t. }\operatorname{trace}(Y^TY)=(1+\alpha)n,\qquad\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb I\{(Y\mathbf1)_i=0\}\le\beta n.
$$
**Remark. weighted kernel NEO-K-means objective**
![](notion-file-block://4b08ef9f-817f-4231-8bac-6a1a31374a62/2e34c5dd-47ed-44b3-8d53-19cac6f2dda4?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-16b.png)
즉, non-exhaustive, overlapping graph clustering에 weighted NEO-K-means 알고리즘을 적용할 수 있다.
$$
\operatorname{dist}(v_i,V_c)=-\frac{2\operatorname{links}(v_i,V_c)}{\deg(v_i)\deg(V_c)}
+\frac{\operatorname{links}(V_c,V_c)}{\deg(V_c)^2}
+\frac\sigma{\deg(v_i)}-\frac\sigma{\deg(V_c)}.
$$
### Multilevel graph clustering
Coarsening → Base clustering → Refinement ($`NC(G)`$).
- Coarsening: merge two nodes and create a super node. ⇒ Create series of graphs: $`G_1,\ldots,G_\ell`$.

### Implication
Let $`\widehat A\equiv AFD_e^{-1}A^T`$: nodes × nodes similarity matrix.
$$
\widehat a_{ij}=\sum_{e\in Q_e}\frac{f(e)}{\deg(e)},\qquad
Q_e=\{e\in E:v_i\in e,v_j\in e\}.
$$
$`v_i,v_j`$를 모두 포함하는 hyperedge만 고려.

### Normalized mutual information (NMI)
$$
NMI(S,C)=\frac{2I(S;C)}{H(S)+H(C)}.
$$
$`H(\cdot)`$: entropy. $`I(S;C)`$: mutual information.
Given discrete random variable $`X`$ with $`x_1,\ldots,x_n`$,
$$
H(X)=-\sum_{i=1}^nP(x_i)\log_2P(x_i).
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I(X;Y)&\equiv H(X)-H(X|Y)\equiv H(Y)-H(Y|X)\\
&\equiv H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)\equiv H(X,Y)-H(X|Y)-H(Y|X).
\end{aligned}
$$
Measure of the mutual dependence.
$$
H(Y|X=x)=-\sum_yP(Y=y|X=x)\log_2P(Y=y|X=x),
$$
$$
H(Y|X)\equiv\sum_xP(x)H(Y|X=x).
$$
$`0\le NMI\le1`$: 0 → no mutual info / 1 → perfect correlation.
NMI ↑ ⇒ better clustering.
Algorithmic과 ground-truth cluster 수가 달라도 NMI 계산 가능. ⇒ $`0\log0\approx0`$.
**Others…**
- Normalized cut ↓ ⇒ better.
- Modularity ↑ ⇒ better.
Ground-truth cluster 몰라도 가능.

## DeepWalk
$`G=(V,E)`$. Goal: learn $`Z\in\mathbb R^{d\times|V|}`$ where $`d`$ is the dim of the latent space. $`z_u`$: embedding vector of node $`u`$.
To measure the similarity, compute **random walks**.
Given starting nodes, do random walks with length $`t`$. Create seq of visited nodes for every node.
![](notion-file-block://7fc3b6d9-54be-4a44-b3ae-b88c7a0d656a/997f5107-b45d-4652-b617-3c502b0c9ded?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-21a.png)
**Word embedding**
비슷한 단어를 close embedding vector로 인코딩하는 것이 목표.
Language models estimate the likelihood of a specific sequence of words appearing in a corpus.
Ex. Skip-Gram → maximizes the co-occurrence probability among the words.
**Random walk-based node embedding**
$`P_R(v|u)`$: estimate the probability of visiting node $`v`$ on a random walk starting from node $`u`$ using random walk strategy.
모든 노드쌍 고려 ×, random walks에 co-occur하는 쌍만 고려.
Idea: map nearby nodes → similar embedding vectors.
![](notion-file-block://710ee51e-df7d-4d17-8571-cad938a26963/59dd053b-7170-40bd-8d88-82a89fab1255?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=2aa65435-c308-4bb9-af38-c6e6497fca7c.png)
1. $`R`$은 사용해서 $`u`$에서 short fixed-length random walks 실행.
2. For each node $`u`$, collect $`N_R(u)`$. ($`N_R(u)`$는 요소 중복 가능.)
3. Optimize by $`\max_f\sum_{u\in V}\log P(N_R(u)|z_u)`$.
$$
\max_f\sum_{u\in V}\log P(N_R(u)|z_u)=\min_f\sum_{u\in V}\sum_{v\in N_R(u)}-\log P(v|z_u).
$$
Note. Softmax function $`\sigma(x)`$:
$$
\sigma(x)_i=\frac{e^{x_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^\ell e^{x_j}},\qquad
P(v|z_u)=\frac{\exp(z_u^Tz_v)}{\sum_{w\in V}\exp(z_u^Tz_w)}.
$$
![](notion-file-block://7922c4bf-e4fd-4261-ac1e-65a2b965f941/417659a9-af16-4d48-a8c1-42812f2ca86a?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-22a.png)
Instead of normalizing all nodes, just $`k`$ random negative samples.
$$
\log\left(\frac{\exp(z_u^Tz_v)}{\sum_{w\in V}\exp(z_u^Tz_w)}\right)
\approx\log S(z_u^Tz_v)-\sum_{i=1}^k\log S(z_u^Tz_{w_i}).
$$
$`S(x)`$: sigmoid function $`=1/(1+e^{-x})`$.
Sample $`k`$ negative nodes:
- Sample each node with probability proportional to its degree.
- $`k\uparrow`$ → robust estimates.
$$
L\equiv\sum_{u\in V}\sum_{v\in N_R(u)}-\log P(v|z_u).
$$
Minimize by **gradient descent**.
1. Randomly initialize $`z_i`$ for all $`i`$.
2. Iterate until convergence. For all $`i`$, compute $`\partial L/\partial z_i`$,
$$
z_i\leftarrow z_i-\eta\frac{\partial L}{\partial z_i},\qquad\eta:\text{learning rate}.
$$
**Stochastic gradient descent**: instead of evaluating gradients over all, evaluate it for each individual training example.
$$
L^{(u)}\equiv\sum_{v\in N_R(u)}-\log P(v|z_u).
$$
1. Randomly initialize $`z_i`$, $`\forall i`$.
2. Iterate until convergence; sample a node $`i`$, $`\forall j`$ calculate $`\partial L^{(i)}/\partial z_j`$.
For all $`j`$, $`z_j\leftarrow z_j-\eta\dfrac{\partial L^{(i)}}{\partial z_j}`$.
### Summary of DeepWalk
1. Run short fixed-length random walks starting from each node.
2. For each $`u`$, collect $`N_R(u)`$, the multi-set of nodes visited on random walks starting from $`u`$.
3. Optimize using gradient descent.
$$
L=\sum_{u\in V}\sum_{v\in N_R(u)}-\log P(v|z_u).
$$
We can approximate this using negative sampling.
## node2vec
DeepWalk ⇒ $`N_R(u)`$: the multiset of nodes visited on random walks starting from $`u`$ using the **standard random walk** $`R`$.
node2vec ⇒ **2nd order random walk** $`R`$ to define $`N_R(u)`$.
Consider both BFS & DFS. Defines a flexible notion of nbhd $`N_R(u)`$ to explore diverse neighbors.
![](notion-file-block://e2182b91-a453-42e0-aff5-d3f08bdd8558/d5a62682-b460-43f7-bf4a-db12328abc29?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-23a.png)
$`u`$ & $`s_1`$ belong to same cluster. $`u`$ & $`s_6`$ share the same structural role.
⇒ BFS, DFS 모두 nbhd를 정의하기 위해 사용.
가능:
- **Homophily**: nodes in the same cluster should be embedded closely.
- **Structural equivalence**: nodes that have similar structural roles should be embedded closely.
### Interpolating BFS and DFS — random walks
Given a node $`u`$, consider a random walk of a fixed length $`\ell`$.
$`c_i`$: $`i`$-th node in the walk.
$`\pi_{vx}`$: unnormalized transition probability from $`v`$ to $`x`$. $`Z`$: normalizing constant.
$$
P(c_i=x\mid c_{i-1}=v)=\begin{cases}\pi_{vx}/Z&(v,x)\in E,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
$$
Consider a random walk that traversed $`(t,v)`$ and now in $`v`$. The next step:
1. Going back to $`t`$.
2. Go another direct neighbor of $`t`$.
3. Go further from $`t`$.
![](notion-file-block://5002a138-4970-49e5-939b-15ee575ea789/59969a52-58a3-4e96-bfea-c14f09af809f?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-24a.png)
$`\alpha`$: search bias.
① $`p`$: return parameter.
- $`p\uparrow`$: avoids 2-hop redundancy.
- $`p\downarrow`$: keep the walk local close to starting node.
② Walk: $`(t,v)\to(v,x)`$, $`x`$ is a direct neighbor of $`t`$.
홉이동의 probability 합이 1이어야 해서 여기서는 parameter 설정 ×.
③ $`q`$: walk-away parameter.
- $`q>1`$: walk is biased towards nodes close to node $`t`$.
- $`q<1`$: visit nodes which are further away from node $`t`$.
Given walk $`(t,v)`$, we consider the next step in terms of $`t`$.
$`(t,v)\to(v,x)`$, and $`x`$ is interpreted in terms of $`t`$.
2nd order random walk with two parameters $`p`$ & $`q`$.
BFS-like walk: low $`p`$. DFS-like walk: low $`q`$.
### node2vec algorithm
1. Compute the random walk transition probabilities.
2. Simulate $`r`$ random walks of length $`\ell`$ starting from each node $`u`$.
3. Optimize using stochastic gradient descent.
$$
L=\sum_{u\in V}\sum_{v\in N_R(u)}-\log P(v|z_u).
$$
DeepWalk와의 차이는 $`N_R(u)`$를 정의하는 게 다름. DeepWalk는 node2vec이 $`p=1,q=1`$인 상황.
### Multi-label classification
- Spectral clustering: take top $`d`$ eigenvectors of the normalized Laplacian matrix of the given graph.
- DeepWalk: simulating uniform random walks.
- LINE: to learn $`d`$-dim feature vector, learn $`d/2`$-dim by BFS-style simulation and learn next $`d/2`$-dim by sampling nodes at a 2-hop distant nodes.
We should train a classifier using learned features.
### Summary of node2vec
Define a random walk that can trade off between BFS & DFS.
$`p,q`$ parameter로 BFS와 DFS를 조절.

### Supervised training
$$
\min_\theta\sum_{(v,y)\in T}L(y,f(z_v)).
$$

$$
J(\theta)=\sum_{v\in V}y_v\log\sigma(z_v^T\theta)+(1-y_v)\log(1-\sigma(z_v^T\theta)).
$$
$`y_v`$: node label. $`z_v^T`$: encoder output. $`\theta`$: classification weight. $`\sigma`$: sigmoid function.

### Graph Attention Network (GAT)
Visual attention: focus on a certain region which provides clues to predict a missing image patch.
![](notion-file-block://153d5b62-187f-4187-845b-a87bf5a0edee/93922ba0-1691-4df3-a0a4-38a9b75e0298?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-30a.png)
$`\alpha_{vu}`$ := weight factor (importance) of node $`u`$'s message to $`v`$ $`=1/|N(v)|`$.
Idea: specify different weights to different neighbors.
Attention mechanism을 포함한 부분만 다름.
![](notion-file-block://b5bfe59c-91f2-494d-962f-fce858f8966d/1bf91483-3052-424a-a977-930dd05683e3?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-30b-final.png)
Normalize $`e_{vu}`$ values to get $`\alpha_{vu}`$.
Use softmax function so that $`\sum_{u\in N(v)}\alpha_{vu}=1`$.
$$
\alpha_{vu}=\frac{\exp(e_{vu})}{\sum_{k\in N(v)}\exp(e_{vk})}.
$$
**Multi-head attention**: stabilize the learning process.
Ex.
$$
\begin{aligned}
h_v^{(\ell)}[1]&=\sum_{u\in N(v)}\alpha_{vu}^1W_\ell h_u^{(\ell-1)},\\
h_v^{(\ell)}[2]&=\sum_{u\in N(v)}\alpha_{vu}^2W_\ell h_u^{(\ell-1)},\\
h_v^{(\ell)}[3]&=\sum_{u\in N(v)}\alpha_{vu}^3W_\ell h_u^{(\ell-1)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
h_v^{(\ell)}=AGG(h_v^{(\ell)}[1],h_v^{(\ell)}[2],h_v^{(\ell)}[3]).
$$
**Static attention**: ranking of attention coefficients is global, unconditioned on query node.
GAT:
$$
e_{vu}=\sigma\left(a_\ell^T[W_\ell h_u^{(\ell-1)},W_\ell h_v^{(\ell-1)}]\right).
$$
$`W_\ell,a_\ell`$ applied consecutively ⇒ collapsed into a single linear layer.
⇒ It has a static attention problem.
**Dynamic attention**: every query has a different ranking of attention coefficients of the keys.
GAT v2:
$$
e_{vu}=a_\ell^T\sigma\left(\widehat W_\ell[h_u^{(\ell-1)},h_v^{(\ell-1)}]\right)
=a_\ell^T\sigma\left(W'_\ell h_u^{(\ell-1)}+W_\ell h_v^{(\ell-1)}\right).
$$

### Batch normalization
Batch of node embeddings가 들어오면, re-center, re-scale함.
Input: $`X\in\mathbb R^{N\times D}`$: $`N`$개 node embeddings.
Output: $`Y\in\mathbb R^{N\times D}`$: normalized node embeddings.
Compute mean and variance over $`N`$ embeddings.
$$
\mu_j=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^NX_{ij},\qquad\sigma_j^2=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N(X_{ij}-\mu_j)^2.
$$

① **Node-level prediction**
Given $`d`$-dim node embeddings $`\{h_v^{(L)}\in\mathbb R^d,\forall v\in V\}`$, we make a $`k`$-way prediction.
Classify: $`k`$ categories / regression: $`k`$ targets.
![](notion-file-block://8b5b8dd4-bdb0-4f9d-a6f0-a2933dfd7a07/cd09a5a0-2af9-4419-963c-6980315ee2a9?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-34c-final.png)
② **Edge-level prediction**
$$
\widehat y_{uv}=\operatorname{Head}_{\mathrm{node}}(h_u^{(L)},h_v^{(L)})
=NN(\operatorname{Concat}(h_u^{(L)},h_v^{(L)})).
$$
![](notion-file-block://800e1081-68c8-40c0-9053-4f62f2bec63e/69ba4c3a-7860-4e38-8c15-9a58ef77b2c4?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=277d5ed7-be04-449b-90be-a4a12faba0ae.png)
$$
\operatorname{Head}_{\mathrm{node}}(h_u^{(L)},h_v^{(L)})=(h_u^{(L)})^Th_v^{(L)}.
$$
← One-way prediction에 적합. Ex. link prediction (존재 유무).
⇒ Extending dot product to $`k`$-way prediction.
$$
\widehat y_{uv}^{(1)}=(h_u^{(L)})^TW^{(1)}h_v^{(L)},\quad\ldots,\quad
\widehat y_{uv}^{(k)}=(h_u^{(L)})^TW^{(k)}h_v^{(L)},
$$
$$
\widehat y_{uv}=\operatorname{Concat}(\widehat y_{uv}^{(1)},\ldots,\widehat y_{uv}^{(k)})\in\mathbb R^k.
$$
③ **Graph-level prediction**
Using all node embedding vectors.
![](notion-file-block://c35917cf-140b-44d2-9fc7-b31ae304bfaf/97063aaa-0357-40c8-9673-4475933e17ba?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-35b.png)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Global mean pooling: }\widehat y_G&=\operatorname{Mean}(\{h_v^{(L)}\in\mathbb R^d,\forall v\in V\}),\\

\text{Global sum pooling: }\widehat y_G&=\operatorname{Sum}(\{h_v^{(L)}\in\mathbb R^d,\forall v\in V\}).

# Ch. 8 Knowledge Graph Embedding
Triplet (head entity, relation, tail entity).
Knowledge graph $`G=(V,R,E)`$: $`V`$ entities, $`R`$ relations, $`E`$ triplets.
Head나 tail을 예측.
Knowledge graph embedding: represent the entities and relations in a low-dimensional feature space.
단계:
1. Represent entities & relations.
2. Define a scoring function $`f_r(h,t)`$ ← plausibility 측정 (타당성).
3. Learn entity and relation representations.
**Translational distance model**: use distance-based scoring function.
Ex. TransE, TransH, TransR, RotatE.
**Semantic matching model**: exploit similarity-based scoring function.
Ex. RESCAL, DistMult, HolE.
## TransE
$`h+r\approx t`$.
![](notion-file-block://1090b530-e3f0-4e69-b8d4-892046bfcf84/5d877110-c083-4846-b32d-f827555f47fb?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-37a.png)
$$
f_r(h,t)=-\|h+r-t\|.
$$
1 to N, N to 1, N to N 관계에 부적절.
즉, 만약 1 to N이면 $`(h,r,t_i)`$에 대해 $`t_1\approx t_2\approx\cdots`$로 분석해버림.
## TransH
Allow an entity to have distinct representations when involved in different relations.
Project onto the hyperplane:
$$
h_\perp=h-w_r^Th\,w_r,\qquad t_\perp=t-w_r^Tt\,w_r.
$$
⇒ $`h_\perp+r\approx t_\perp`$.
$`w_r`$: unit normal vector of the hyperplane of relation $`r`$.
## TransR
Introduce relation-specific spaces.
![](notion-file-block://2e9c8944-4522-46e1-8a1c-a5118d384050/c085fcc5-eff8-4bdc-8221-3844693f391d?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-37b.png)
$$
h_r=M_rh,\qquad t_r=M_rt.
$$
$`M_r\in\mathbb R^{k\times d}`$: projection matrix from the entity space to the relation space of $`r`$.
$$
f_r(h,t)=-\|h_r+r-t_r\|.
$$
## RotatE
Maps the entities and relations to the complex vector space & defines each relation as a rotation from the source entity to the target entity.
![](notion-file-block://40af9f22-60be-4deb-8588-fdac5a1484d4/415465c4-92ff-4b58-95b5-26bc9a7cab59?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-37c.png)
$`h\circ r\approx t`$.
RotatE는 symmetric relation을 처리 가능.
![](notion-file-block://1dcb7f5f-9ced-449a-8192-767e4b271b2d/077ae2ed-8402-42b5-a609-4fc2d21010d0?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-37d.png)
## RESCAL
Associate each entity with a vector to capture its latent semantics.
Each relation is represented as a matrix ⇒ models pairwise interactions between all the components of $`h`$ and $`t`$.
![](notion-file-block://e0713d22-f9f0-4c26-bfa1-d594022975e6/d6bfefb1-c930-4831-be0a-4232efe0f005?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-37e.png)
$$
f_r(h,t)=h^TM_rt=\sum_{i=1}^d\sum_{j=1}^d[M_r]_{ij}\,[h]_i\,[t]_j.
$$
## DistMult
RESCAL에서 $`M_r`$을 diagonal matrix로 간단화.
![](notion-file-block://b6865d3a-9503-4338-bf1f-9432b4209cba/2618064f-aba3-4b96-85d7-e6fafcea4da6?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-38a.png)
$$
f_r(h,t)=h^T\operatorname{diag}(r)t=\sum_{i=1}^d[r]_i\,[h]_i\,[t]_i.
$$
Same dimension의 $`h,t`$ 사용.
$`h^T\operatorname{diag}(r)t=t^T\operatorname{diag}(r)h`$이므로 symmetric relation만 다룸.
## HolE
RESCAL의 expressive power와 DistMult의 efficiency 모두 가짐.
![](notion-file-block://bacfb4cc-2436-44ab-a81a-60891ae65468/f94dc435-fa05-49b4-9f26-86221349bd7f?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-38b.png)
Composing $`h\star t\in\mathbb R^d`$.
Circular correlation operation:
$$
[h\star t]_i=\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}[h]_k\,[t]_{(k+i)\bmod d}.
$$
$$
f_r(h,t)=r^T(h\star t)=\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}[r]_i\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}[h]_k\,[t]_{(k+i)\bmod d}.
$$
## Negative sampling
Generating negative training examples.

## Knowledge graph embedding with GNNs
### Relational GCN (RGCN)
Average info from nbrs and apply a neural network.
만약 relation type이 다르면 각각 다른 neural network weight 적용.
Add reversed relations for message passing.
![](notion-file-block://3648b3ee-2597-45e1-9b02-bf7b70feba64/0ce3a131-e3ae-48b8-88e6-164e76668697?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-39b.png)
Each relation has $`L`$ matrices: $`W_r^{(1)},\ldots,W_r^{(L)}`$.
Size of $`W_r^{(\ell)}`$ = $`d^{(\ell+1)}\times d^{(\ell)}`$ ← hidden dimension in layer $`\ell`$.
Relation이 많아지면 parameter수가 급격히 많아짐.
Regularize the weights $`W_r^{(\ell)}`$: ① Block-diagonal decomposition, ② Basis decomposition.
① **Block-diagonal decomposition** ⇒ make the weights sparse.
$$
d^{(\ell+1)}\times d^{(\ell)}\quad\to\quad B\times\frac{d^{(\ell+1)}}B\times\frac{d^{(\ell)}}B.
$$
② **Basis decomposition** ⇒ share weights across different relations.
Matrix of each relation as a linear combination of basis transformations.
$$
W_r=\sum_{b=1}^Ba_{rb}V_b.
$$
$`V_b`$: $`B`$ basis matrices, shared across all relations.
$`a_{rb}`$: importance weight of matrix $`V_b`$. ⇒ $`\{a_{rb}\}_{b=1}^B`$만 학습하면 됨.
## Link prediction
RGCN: encoder, DistMult: decoder.
$`\forall r`$ is associated with a diagonal matrix $`R_r`$ ($`d\times d`$).
![](notion-file-block://dc87456a-590a-467b-a6c0-6f449c28af80/f3fc5c16-d479-4388-9175-921d20233b95?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=9551279b-7284-4a69-95a7-ff92439c6984.png)
$`(E,r_3,A)`$: supervision triplet 가정 (감독).
$`h_E^{(L)},h_A^{(L)}`$을 통해 score function 계산.
DistMult:
$$
f_{r_3}(h_E,h_A)=h_E^TW_{r_3}h_A.
$$
**Training**
1. $`(E,r_3,A)`$: training supervision triplet 가정.
2. Negative sampling ($`\ne`$ training triplet).
3. RGCN → represent node embedding.
4. DistMult → represent relations. Optimize margin-based ranking loss.
![](notion-file-block://8ce538d1-d2fd-4edd-831b-d695304245da/89e420f6-9d48-49ab-9c3b-2fd086eb08d3?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-40b.png)
**Evaluation**
Predict validation of $`(E,r_3,D)`$.
Score should be higher than $`(E,r_3,v)`$, $`\forall v`$ where $`(E,r_3,v)`$ is not in training triplets.
⇒ Obtain ranking of $`(E,r_3,D)`$ → calculate metrics.
Ex. MR, MRR, Hit @ n.
## Evaluation & applications
### Benchmark dataset
- Freebase: community members에 의해서 자유롭게 만들어짐.
- NELL (Never-Ending Language Learning): semantic relation.
- WordNet: lexical DB of semantic relations.
### Data splitting
1. Train set: optimize model parameters, learn the embeddings.
2. Validation set: used for tuning hyperparameters.
3. Test set: used for testing performance.
Link prediction:
- Entity prediction: $`(?,r,t)`$, $`(h,r,?)`$.
- Relation prediction: $`(h,?,t)`$.
$`(?,r,t)`$에서 그냥 모든 $`h`$에 대해 score 구하고 ranking으로 예측 가능.
TransE를 사용해서 embedding을 학습한다면,
$$
f_r(h,t)=-\|h+r-t\|.
$$
Evaluation할 때 $`(h,r,t)\in\text{test set}`$.
⇒ Make $`(?,r,t)`$, $`(h,r,?)`$ problems.
⇒ Score ranking.
⇒ Record ranks of correct answers in the ordered lists.
1. **Mean Rank (MR)** → 평균 랭킹 계산, lower the better.
2. **Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)** → 역수 랭킹 평균 계산, higher better.
3. **Hit @ N** → proportion of ranks no larger than $`N`$, higher better.
MR vs MRR: MR은 랭킹이 많이 바뀔 때, MRR은 top-ranked가 바뀔 때 많이 바뀜.
With filtered metrics: some problem has many solutions.
다른 정답인 애들을 빼고 랭킹 고려.

**Matrix factorization**
![](notion-file-block://ef593599-1d84-42bf-90ae-db41a77d1e42/25c8fdb5-55c5-42ef-a4e8-2bd744c49106?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-42b.png)
$$
R\approx UM^T,\qquad R_{ij}\approx u_i^Tm_j.
$$
Col of $`U`$: latent (잠재의) factors for users.
Col of $`M`$: latent factors for movies.
Row $`i`$ of $`U`$: user $`i`$'s low-dim representation.
Row $`j`$ of $`M`$: movie $`j`$'s low-dim representation.
How to find $`u_i,m_j`$?
$$
\min_{u_1,\ldots,u_m,\,m_1,\ldots,m_n}\sum_{(i,j)\in S}(R_{ij}-u_i^Tm_j)^2
+\lambda\sum_i\|u_i\|_2^2+\lambda\sum_j\|m_j\|_2^2
$$
$$
\equiv\|W\odot(R-UM^T)\|_F^2+\lambda(\|U\|_F^2+\|M\|_F^2).
$$
$`W_{ij}=1`$ if $`(i,j)\in S`$ (known rating), otherwise 0.
$`\odot`$: elementwise product.
**Alternating minimization**: fix $`U`$ and compute $`M`$, then fix $`M`$ and compute $`U`$, …반복.
When we fix $`M`$, $`R^T\approx MU^T`$.
이것과 다음 문제 번갈아 푸는구나 → missing value 무시.
$$
\min_{u_1,\ldots,u_m}\sum_{i=1}^m\|r_i-Mu_i\|_2^2+\lambda\sum_{i=1}^m\|u_i\|_2^2
\quad\text{: Ridge Regression}.
$$
![](notion-file-block://b312a975-439f-4a98-b457-1ed82c9c02ec/ce32884c-542b-432e-829c-5b01683e143c?space_id=a572f2a2-f7b0-4923-b5c0-74f5db67bcd1&name=cs471-43a.png)
Preference of user $`i`$ to item $`j`$ ⇒ $`u_i^Tm_j`$.
Ranking optimization over pairwise preference ⇒ used to learn latent vectors.
$$
i:j_1>j_2>\cdots>j_n\quad\Rightarrow\quad
u_i^Tm_{j_1}>u_i^Tm_{j_2}>\cdots>u_i^Tm_{j_n}.
$$
