Introduction

Because of KL divergence constraint, TRPO is complicated and is not compatible with noise or parameter sharing. This paper introduces an algorithm that attains the data efficiency and reliable performance of TRPO, while using only 1st-order optimization, proposing a novel objective with clipped probability ratios.

Clipped Surrogate Objective

(b)    LCPI(θ)=E^t[πθ(at∣st)πθold(at∣st)A^t]=E^t[rt(θ)A^t](b) \ \ \ \ L^\text{CPI}(\theta)=\hat{\mathbb{E}}_t \left[ \frac{\pi_\theta(a_t \mid s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(a_t \mid s_t)} \hat{A}_t \right]=\hat{\mathbb{E}}_t \left[ r_t(\theta)\hat{A}_t \right](b)    LCPI(θ)=E^t​[πθold​​(at​∣st​)πθ​(at​∣st​)​A^t​]=E^t​[rt​(θ)A^t​]

CPI: conservative policy iteration in TRPO.
Without a constraint, (b) would lead to an excessively large policy update. Hence this paper proposes the following with hyperparameter ϵ\epsilonϵ:

LCLIP(θ)=E^t[min⁡(rt(θ)A^t, clip(rt(θ),1−ϵ,1+ϵ)A^t)]L^{\text{CLIP}}(\theta) = \hat{\mathbb{E}}_t \left[ \min(r_t(\theta)\hat{A}_t, \, \text{clip}(r_t(\theta), 1 - \epsilon, 1 + \epsilon)\hat{A}_t) \right]LCLIP(θ)=E^t​[min(rt​(θ)A^t​,clip(rt​(θ),1−ϵ,1+ϵ)A^t​)]

It removes the incentive for moving rtr_trt​ outside of the interval [1−ϵ,1+ϵ][1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon][1−ϵ,1+ϵ]. Since we take the minimum of the clipped and unclipped objective, so it is a lower bound on the unclipped objective, which guarantees a lower bound of policy performance.
